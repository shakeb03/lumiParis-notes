# Word of Mouth and Urgency

## Direction
Quality wear with a subtle, quiet-luxury feel. Internal shorthand: "old money". **Never use that phrase, or "heritage", "heirloom", "est." or "since", in public copy.** Growth comes from people talking about the pieces, in the way The Ordinary grew: restraint, plain facts, no hype. The brand stays calm ([[Voice and Rules]]).

## Rule on urgency
Shoppers may feel that pieces are worth acting on. They are never led to believe something false: no invented stock, no false limits, no staged sell-outs, no hidden countdowns, no "x people viewing". Implied scarcity that isn't real counts as a false claim, so every signal below is a true fact. Because Branvas's catalog is shared, we never imply a piece is exclusive or only available here.

## True levers (nothing here says "limited stock")
| Feeling | Real mechanism |
|---|---|
| These pieces are chosen | Small, curated catalog (a fact by design) per [[Quality Gate]] |
| New things arrive and move on | Named seasonal edits. When a piece is retired (by us or by Branvas), it moves to an "Archive" page, quietly |
| It might go | Real stock sync from Branvas. When a style is out, the page says so and offers "Notify me" |
| Be first | Early access list: subscribers see new edits before the public. True, and said plainly as a benefit |
| Gifts have deadlines | Real Christmas order-by dates, padded from Branvas windows ([[Branvas]]). The strongest true urgency for the 50k plan |
| Others have it | Real reviews and customer photos only, with permission. AI images are never shown as customers |

## Word of mouth
- **Make it easy to pass on:** gift-ready packaging (Branvas ribboned box, tissue, cloth), a gifting edit, "Give the light." at Christmas
- **Referral:** a personal share code or link for each customer, built from Shopify's built-in discounts. Credit amount and mechanic: founder decides #founder. Check any app cost against the free-tools budget ([[Budget]])
- **Early access list:** sign-up on the site and in bios; first look at each edit
- **Reviews:** ask every buyer for a review after delivery. Real only
- **Pinterest and saves:** pins built to be saved and shared ([[Socials Hub]])
- **Tone:** no popups shouting, no countdown banners, no discount banners

## Never
Fake or hidden scarcity, "limited", "only X left", "selling fast", "exclusive", "last chance", staged out-of-stocks, fake countdowns, invented waitlist numbers, or retiring a bestseller just to create scarcity.
