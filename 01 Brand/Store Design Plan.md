# Store Design Plan (planning only, nothing built yet)

Goal: a mobile-native, calm, high-converting store. Traffic comes from TikTok, Instagram and Pinterest, nearly all on phones, often inside each app's own browser. Brand rules: [[Brand Bible]], [[Voice and Rules]], [[Word of Mouth and Urgency]]. Setup steps: [[Shopify Setup]].

## What the competitors do (read 2026-10-03, from page text; not seen visually on a phone)
Reviewed: Mejuri, Missoma, Monica Vinader, Ana Luisa, Gorjana, Astrid & Miyu, EVRY Jewels, En Route, Catbird. Aurate's site could not be read.
- **Two camps.** Quiet and editorial: Mejuri, Monica Vinader, Gorjana, Catbird (little urgency, lifestyle imagery, collection-led). Discount-driven: EVRY, En Route, Ana Luisa, Astrid & Miyu in part (countdowns, "selling fast", "low stock", "last chance", sitewide sales). **We follow the quiet camp.**
- **Common structure:** announcement bar, sticky header with search, wishlist and cart, one campaign hero, category cards, best sellers row, editorial or values block, reviews, email signup, footer.
- **Navigation:** Earrings, Necklaces, Bracelets, Rings, Best Sellers or New, Gifts.
- **Trust:** warranty (2 to 5 years at Mejuri, Missoma, Ana Luisa, Monica Vinader), free returns (30 to 100 days), free delivery with a threshold, payment wallets and installments (Monica Vinader).
- **Product cards:** price always visible, material named in the title or card ("18k Gold Plated"), quick add, small badges (New). Review counts on some.
- **Product page (Mejuri):** about 5 images, title with rating, price and material selector, three reassurance badges (warranty, free returns, free shipping), "style with" row, short description, materials accordion.
- **Email capture:** most offer 10% off. Gorjana, Ana Luisa and Mejuri do not lead with a discount. We use an early access list with no discount.
- **Imagery:** soft light, neutral grounds, hand, wrist and ear close-ups, stacked looks. This matches our plan.
- **Watch:** Mejuri's line "jewelry you can live in" is close to ours ("meant to be lived in"). Keep our wording distinct.
- **Do not copy:** claims like "waterproof" and "hypoallergenic" (they sell solid gold; we cannot say it).

## Our advantages and gaps
- Advantage: complimentary shipping on every order, no threshold. Competitors set thresholds.
- Gap: no warranty. Competitors offer change-of-mind returns for 30 days or more; we now offer 7 days for store credit, shorter and credit-only, so state it clearly on the product page. Damaged, defective or incorrect items get a free replacement
- Gap: no reviews at launch, and we never fake them. Collect real ones from the first orders.
- Fill the gap with: exact material facts, delivery windows, gift-ready box, fast support reply, and calm clarity.

## Principles (mobile first)
- One column, big images, thumb-reach buttons (at least 44px), short copy, few taps from post to checkout
- Product first: each social post links to the product or edit it shows, never the homepage
- Light, fast pages: compressed WebP images, lazy loading, two font weights per family, minimal apps
- No popups, countdowns or discount banners

## Navigation
Shop all · Earrings · Necklaces · Rings · Bracelets · Gifts. Archive in the footer. Sticky header with centred logo, search and bag. Add "New" or "The edit" once there are drops.

## Homepage (mobile order)
1. Announcement bar: delivery window or, for Canadian visitors only, the Canada price line
2. Header
3. Hero: one image of light on skin, "Wear the light.", one button "Shop the edit"
4. Category tiles (4)
5. The edit: 4 to 6 pieces in a swipe row, price visible
6. Three plain facts about materials and care
7. Brand line (the approved name story)
8. Real reviews (hidden until real ones exist)
9. Early access sign-up, inline, no discount
10. Footer: shipping and delivery, returns, care, contact, policies

## Collection page
Two-column grid. Card: image (second image on tap), name, material line, price. Quick add. Filters: category and material. No badges except "New". No sale styling.

## Product page
1. Swipe gallery of 5 to 6 AI images that match the Branvas piece exactly: close-up, worn, layered, still life, gift box
2. Name, price (with "Canada price" label for Canada), short plain description
3. Variant choice, then **Add to bag** (sticky on scroll)
4. Wallets: Shop Pay, Apple Pay, Google Pay. Installments if available for Canada (check)
5. Reassurance row: Complimentary shipping · Delivery 5 to 8 days (US), 7 to 10 (Canada) · 7-day returns for store credit · Gift-ready box
6. Accordions: Details, Materials and care, Shipping and returns
7. "Wear it with" row
8. Size guide for rings and bracelets
9. Real reviews when they exist

## Cart and checkout
Slide-out bag, "Complimentary shipping" line, wallets first, minimal fields, CA$ or US$ always clear. Prices include tax while the business is unregistered.

## Social landing
- Post links go to the exact product or edit. UTM tags on every link
- A simple "link in bio" page: latest edit, bestsellers, early access
- Pinterest product pins and Instagram/TikTok shopping channels set up through Shopify's free channels. Check each is free
- Test every page inside the Instagram, TikTok and Pinterest in-app browsers, not only Safari or Chrome

## Theme (decided 2026-10-03: free theme, restyled hard)
A store looks like dropshipping because of what is on it, not what the theme costs: countdown timers, popups, sale badges and "selling fast" tags, crowded menus, supplier photos, long supplier-style product titles, and mismatched fonts, colours and image styles. Our rules already avoid these.
- **Chosen: a free Shopify theme, restyled hard.** Our fonts, palette, spacing, section order and copy; no clutter; our own AI images; custom sections or small code edits for sticky add to bag, the Canada-only top bar and the product page layout. Needs to be Online Store 2.0, fast, and flexible with sections
- Paid theme: not now. Revisit after the first orders if the free theme falls short
- Switching themes later is possible: products, pages and policies stay; section layouts need redoing
- **Next:** preview 2 or 3 free themes with test products, review on a phone against the checklist below, and pick the quietest base
- **Quiet-store checklist:** no popups or timers; two fonts only; palette only from [[Brand Bible]]; generous white space; one image style; one primary button per screen; short product titles; no supplier wording; tap targets at least 44px; page loads fast on mobile data

Apply Lumi Cream, Ivory Soft, Warm Ink, Cormorant Garamond headlines, Outfit body.

## Test products
Founder adds fake products to test the theme. Rules: name them "TEST - ...", tag `test`, keep them unpublished or behind the store password, no fabricated material claims in them, and delete them all before launch. Never leave a test product on a live page.
