# Voice and Rules

## Voice
Calm, honest, plain. Never hustles, never overclaims. Short sentences. Say what the piece is, how it is worn, how to look after it. Let the light do the selling.

## Lines in use
- **Primary tagline:** Wear the light.
- Gifting season: "Give the light." (proposed)
- Social captions only: "Glow, not glitter."
- Product pages and email: "The quiet kind of shine." (proposed)

## Sample copy (illustrative only; check every claim against the product note)
- Product: "Gold-plated huggie hoops with a single cubic zirconia. Small enough to sleep in, bright enough to notice."
- Care: "Keep pearls dry. Wipe silver with the cloth in the box."
- Shipping: "Complimentary shipping to the US and Canada. Delivery is usually 5 to 8 business days in the US and 7 to 10 in Canada."

## Canada price copy
- Label on Canadian product pages: "Canada price". Line beneath: "The same number as our US price, in Canadian dollars."
- Canada-only top bar (Canadian visitors only): "Canada price: the same number as our US price, in Canadian dollars. Complimentary shipping."
- US visitors see plain US-dollar prices and nothing Canada-specific
- Never: strikethrough, "sale", "discount", "save", "% off", or a before price. The price is the price
- Keep it non-political: no tariffs, trade war or country-versus-country lines. The number does the talking
- No duties-included claim until the first Canadian order confirms Branvas's duties-prepaid delivery

## Always
- "Lumi Paris", exact spelling
- Talk about wearing: daily, layered, lived in
- Use material wording exactly as [[Branvas]] states it. Say "gold-plated", with the base metal as Branvas names it
- Describe, don't hype. One clear claim per sentence
- Placeholders for the address until decided: `[BUSINESS ADDRESS]`, `[PO BOX / RETURNS ADDRESS]`, `[SHIP-FROM LOCATION]`

## Never
- "The Lumi Paris" (outside the domain and Instagram handle)
- French studio, French-made, atelier, "crafted in Paris", "from Paris", "Parisian-made", "Paris, France"
- Eiffel Tower, Haussmann streets or other Paris scenes used as proof of origin
- Invented reviews, stock counts, certifications, origin claims
- "Gold" or "vermeil" for plated pieces; karat or plating thickness unless Branvas states it
- "Waterproof" or "water-safe" except for stainless steel styles Branvas marks water-safe
- "Hypoallergenic", "nickel-free", "tarnish-free", "lifetime", "handmade", "handcrafted" unless Branvas confirms in writing
- "Eco", "sustainable", "ethical" (Branvas calls its brass "eco-brass"; that is a supplier term, not our claim)
- "Diamond" for moissanite or zirconia
- Fake or hidden urgency: countdowns, "selling fast", "last chance", "limited", "only X left", staged sell-outs. Urgency comes only from true facts ([[Word of Mouth and Urgency]])
- "Old money", "heritage", "heirloom", "est.", "since" in public copy
- Words like luxury, exclusive, must-have, obsessed, perfect
- Offering a warranty, or cash refunds for change of mind. Change-of-mind returns are store credit only, within 7 days of delivery ([[Policies]])
- Calling the 50k goal ambitious or hard
- Volunteering that the business is AI-run. Support is AI: if a customer asks, say so plainly. Never claim to be human, never use an invented human name ([[Support Playbook]])
