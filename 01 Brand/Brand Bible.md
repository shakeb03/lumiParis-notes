# Brand Bible

## Identity
- **Name:** Lumi Paris. Never "The Lumi Paris". "the" appears only in the domain (thelumiparis.com) and Instagram handle (the.lumiparis), where unavoidable.
- **Category:** demi-fine jewelry (fashion and demi-fine, not fine jewelry)
- **Positioning:** luminous everyday jewelry, meant to be lived in
- **Tagline:** **Wear the light.** (primary)
- **Logo:** LUMI in a high-contrast serif with a flowing stroke from the U into the M, PARIS beneath in a light, widely spaced sans. Files in [[Asset Index]].
- **Quality and feel:** quality wear, quiet and subtle, quiet-luxury mood. Brand image and consistency outrank catalog size. See [[Quality Gate]] and [[Word of Mouth and Urgency]]
- **Art direction:** light first, logo second. The product and the light carry the image; the logo sits on site header, packaging, avatars.

## Name story (approved line, word for word)
> Lumi comes from lumière: light. Paris is how we look at jewelry, not where we're based.

"Paris" means an edited, restrained eye: calm, quiet-glow pieces chosen for how they catch light, nothing loud or hustling. Claims we never make are in [[Voice and Rules]].

**Rules:** buttons are Warm Ink with Lumi Cream text. Ivory Soft for cards and bands.
**Contrast on Lumi Cream:** Warm Ink 15.7:1, Jardin Green 10.7:1, Stone Text 5.2:1 (4.7:1 on Ivory Soft), Stone Mist 2.3:1 (fails for text), Champagne 2.1:1 (decorative only).

## Typography (approved)
- **Headlines, and any text-set "Lumi Paris" wordmark:** Cormorant Garamond. The logo file stays the master wherever the logo appears.
- **Body text, buttons, prices:** Outfit, normal case.
- **Small labels only:** Outfit in letter-spaced caps.
- If a font is missing in the Shopify theme, use the nearest equivalent and log it in [[Decisions Log]].

## Customer
- Women 18 to 40, buying for themselves and as gifts. Christmas is the main gifting event.
- Markets: United States and Canada. Prices in USD for the US and CAD for Canada.
- Spelling: US "jewelry" in all customer-facing copy.

## Imagery (all AI-generated, no real people, no photoshoots)
- People wearing the pieces, plus close-ups. Light-led, soft natural shadows, skin and fabric in neutral tones that sit inside the palette.
- The piece in every image must match the Branvas product exactly: shape, stone count, colour, size on the body. If the AI render drifts, regenerate.
- No Paris landmarks, no Haussmann streets, no French signage. Paris is a way of looking, not a setting.
- AI people are never presented as customers, reviewers or "real" wearers. Reviews come only from real buyers.
- No text overlays that make claims. Prompt library: [[Imagery Prompts]].

## References (feel to match)
Mejuri, Missoma, Monica Vinader, Ana Luisa, Gorjana, Astrid & Miyu, EVRY Jewels, Aurate, En Route, Catbird.
Avoid: glitzy, discount-style, loud, hustle.

## Brand story (draft for About page)
Lumi comes from lumière: light. Paris is how we look at jewelry, not where we're based. We choose calm, quiet-glow pieces for how they catch light, and leave out anything loud. They are made to be worn every day, not saved for later.
