# Socials Imagery

**Vision:** light-led, varied, platform-native. Same palette and light as the site, but looser and more alive. The aim is a feed people save and share. Pillars and handles: [[Socials Hub]]. Calendar: [[Content Calendar]].

## Named styles (each maps to a pillar)
| Style | Pillar | Look |
|---|---|---|
| **Glow** | Product close-ups | Macro on stone and metal, light catching the facets, shallow depth of field. "Glow, not glitter." |
| **Daily** | Worn daily | Lived-in crops: ear while hair is tucked, wrist on a coffee cup, collarbone under a knit. Natural, unposed |
| **Stack** | Layering | Two or three catalog pieces together, styling ideas, same crop distance |
| **Gift** | Gifting (Nov to Dec) | Box, cloth, ribbon, hand wrapping. "Give the light." No price or claim text |
| **The edit** | Why a piece made the cut | Still life of one piece with a note on how it catches light. Calm, spare |

## Per platform
| Platform | Format | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Instagram | 4:5 at 1080x1350; carousels of 3 to 5 (hero, close-up, worn); Reels 9:16 | Consistent grade so the grid reads as a set. First slide is the cleanest |
| TikTok | 9:16 at 1080x1920, 5 to 8 seconds | Slow push-ins or light sweeps on a still, not busy motion. Credits are limited, so animate winners only |
| Pinterest | 2:3 at 1000x1500 | Boards: Worn daily, Layering, Close-ups, Gifting, Quiet glow. Search-friendly, calm. May carry "Wear the light." and the logo only |

## Rules
- Same light and palette as [[Website Imagery]], but more crops, more life, more variation in subject
- Any jewelry shown is a catalog piece that matches its Branvas photo ([[Product Imagery]] checklist for the featured piece)
- AI people are never presented as customers. No "real customer", "our community" or reposting AI faces as reviews
- No text claims in the image. Captions follow [[Voice and Rules]] and the `social-post` skill
- No fake urgency, countdown or "selling fast" overlay
- Use the platform's AI label where it asks
- Reframe and recrop winners across platforms instead of regenerating

## Batches (to save credits)
Generate by theme, not by post: one Glow batch of 6, one Daily batch of 6, one Stack batch of 4, then crop each to 4:5, 9:16 and 2:3. 9 images per platform to start come from about 12 base generations.

## Prompt block
`[Base style block]` + style line (`Glow: macro, shallow depth of field, light on facets`; `Daily: natural lifestyle crop, unposed, face out of frame`; `Stack: two pieces layered, same distance, calm`; `Gift: box, cloth and ribbon on cream`) + `[CATALOG PIECE exact replica of reference]`
