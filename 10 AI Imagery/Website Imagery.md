# Website Imagery

**Vision:** light on skin. Quiet editorial, the first thing a visitor feels. A small set of images that look like one photographer, one morning, one window. Plan: [[Store Design Plan]], build: [[Store Build Brief]].

## Look
- Soft natural window light from one side, gentle long shadows, a visible soft light streak
- Cream and ivory grounds, warm stone, skin and fabric in neutral tones
- Calm, unposed, a lot of negative space (text sits on a Lumi Cream panel, not over the image)
- One colour grade across every website image: warm, slightly lifted blacks, low contrast, no saturation boost
- Crops tight on body: ear, collarbone, wrist, hand. Faces optional and mostly out of frame
- Feels expensive by restraint, never by props. No flowers, marble or champagne cliches

## Shots (about 12 images)
| Use | Count | Ratio and size | Brief |
|---|---|---|---|
| Home hero | 1 (plus a mobile crop) | 3:2 at 2400x1600; 4:5 at 1200x1500 | Light falling across a collarbone or ear, a single piece visible, empty space on one side. Headline "Wear the light." sits in the panel |
| Category tiles | 4 | 1:1 or 4:5 at 1200 | Earrings, necklaces, rings, bracelets. Identical crop distance, light direction and ground, so the four read as a set |
| The edit row backdrop / editorial band | 1 to 2 | 3:2 | Still life: pieces on cream with a light streak |
| About page | 1 to 2 | 3:2 | Hands, fabric, light. No faces. No studio or workshop scene |
| Gifting page / season band | 1 | 3:2 | Branvas box, cloth and ribbon. No price or claim text |
| Early access signup band | 1 | 3:2 | Quietest image on the site, lots of space |
| Password and 404 page | 1 | 4:5 | Same family, minimal |

## Prompt block
`[Base style block from Imagery Prompts]` + `wide editorial composition, large area of empty cream space on the left, single soft light streak crossing skin, ear and collarbone crop, face out of frame, no text, no logo, no jewelry other than [CATALOG PIECE or none]`

Vary only the subject, never the light or the grade. Keep the winning seed and prompt for the whole set.

## Checklist
- [ ] Reads as the same set as the other website images (light direction, colour grade, ground)
- [ ] Any jewelry shown is a catalog piece that matches its Branvas photo, or the shot has none
- [ ] Space for text is clean (hero panel, tiles)
- [ ] Looks right on a 375px phone crop and on desktop
- [ ] No text, logo, landmark, stock-looking prop or uncanny hand
- [ ] Exported as WebP, hero under 250 KB and others under 150 KB, alt text written ([[Product Names and Descriptions]] rules)
- [ ] Founder approved the look

## Effort
Spend the most creative effort here and the fewest images. Generate 10 or more variants per shot, keep one. Founder judges every website image by look before it goes on the theme.
