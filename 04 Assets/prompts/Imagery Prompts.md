# Imagery Prompts

All imagery is AI-generated. No real people, no photoshoots. Rules: [[Brand Bible]]. Budget: [[Budget]].

## Base style block (paste into every prompt)
Soft natural window light, gentle shadows, warm neutral palette (cream `#F7F2EA`, ivory `#EFE6D8`, warm stone), quiet and uncluttered, shallow depth of field, skin and fabric in neutral tones, editorial but unposed. No text, no logos, no landmarks, no signage.

## Shot types
1. **Close-up:** piece on skin or fabric, light catching stones and metal
2. **Worn:** crop on ear, neck, hand or wrist, face optional
3. **Layered:** two or three pieces together, daily-wear styling
4. **Still life:** piece on cream or ivory surface with a soft light streak
5. **Gifting:** box and polishing cloth from the Branvas packaging, ribbon, no price or claim text

## Accuracy check (every image, before it is approved)
- [ ] Piece matches the Branvas product photo: shape, stone count, colour, scale
- [ ] No extra jewelry that is not for sale, unless it is also in the catalog
- [ ] No Paris landmarks or French signage
- [ ] Hands, ears and fingers look natural
- [ ] File named `lumi_<product>_<type>_<yyyymmdd>_v1` and logged in [[Asset Index]]

## Credit rules (limited AI credits)
- Test one product per shot type before batches
- Prefer stills over video until a style shows traction
- Reuse winners across Instagram, TikTok and Pinterest; reframe instead of regenerating
- Log prompt and result in this folder so a winning prompt is never lost
