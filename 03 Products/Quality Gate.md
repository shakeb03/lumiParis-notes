# Quality Gate

Brand image and consistency come first. A piece that could damage the reputation is not listed. A small catalog is fine; quality over count. Every piece must pass every check, and the founder approves each one by look #founder.

## Look
- Quiet, classic, subtle: thin chains, small hoops and huggies, pearl and stud basics, simple pendants, delicate stacking rings
- Fits the palette and the "quiet glow" idea ([[Brand Bible]])
- Skip: novelty motifs (skulls, animals, cartoons), oversized or multicolour stones, visible logos, trend gimmicks, anything that reads loud
- Must look like a $129.99 piece in the Branvas photo. If the photo already looks cheap, it fails

## Material (from Branvas product page)
- Preferred: S925 sterling silver, gold-plated S925, stainless steel, moissanite set in S925, freshwater pearl on S925 or steel
- Gold-plated brass or zinc alloy: only by exception, founder approves, and not as a ring
- Base metal, plating and dimensions must be stated by Branvas. No spec, no listing. Ask Branvas first
- Nickel content unknown: do not list. Never claim hypoallergenic

## Evidence
- No samples are ordered, so the gate rests on Branvas specs and photos. Treat the first real orders as the check ([[Branvas]])
- Rollout: launch a small first group. Expand only after the first orders arrive in good shape

## Retire rule (proposed)
- Pause a style on 2 damaged, defective or "not as described" claims within its first 20 orders
- Retire a style Branvas retires or that fails repeatedly. Log in [[Decisions Log]]
