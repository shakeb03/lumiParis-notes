## Launch capsule (proposed, founder to judge the look #founder)
Every style must pass the [[Quality Gate]]. Fewer is fine: quality over count. Up to 24 styles that read as one quiet-glow collection; the Growth plan allows 75, so there is room later.
- Start from these Branvas collections: **Sterling Luna** (S925 silver), **Sterling Glow** (gold-plated S925), **Steel Edit** (water-safe stainless steel), **Moissanite Spark** (moissanite in S925), **Feminine Aura** (soft, dainty zirconia and pearl)
- Categories: earrings (studs, huggies, small hoops), necklaces (pendants, chains), rings (stackable, adjustable), bracelets. Skip pins and brooches at launch
- Skip: gothic, skull, statement and "Command", "Rebel Sugar", "Dark Romance", "Prism Party", "Vintage Boho" styles, and all men's collections
- Include a gifting set or two (matching sets)
- Include at least 4 water-safe stainless steel styles so we can say "everyday, even in the shower" truthfully for those only

## Pricing rules
- **Base price: $129.99. No piece is listed below it.** If we love a piece and it costs Branvas $20, it lists at $129.99
- **Branvas unit cost up to $75:** list at $129.99
- **Branvas unit cost above $75:** list at unit cost + 75% (cost x 1.75). Shipping is not part of the 75%. Round to a .99 ending
- Shipping is complimentary to the customer. We absorb Branvas's flat $8.99 per order
- Branvas suggested retail is a reference only. We set our own prices
- Currency: USD for the US, CAD for Canada (Shopify Markets with fixed prices per market, confirm in setup). Branvas costs are in USD
- **Same-number pricing:** every product has the same number in both countries: US$X in the US, CA$X in Canada. Canada shows a "Canada price" label and a Canada-only top bar, never a strikethrough or "sale". No exceptions, so the label is always true
- Re-check the exchange rate monthly. If the Canadian dollar weakens, Canada contribution shrinks

## Material fit at this price (proposed)
Branvas's catalog is shared with other merchants, so a shopper can find the same piece cheaper. At $129.99 the launch capsule should lean on stronger materials: S925 sterling silver, gold-plated S925, stainless steel (water-safe), moissanite in S925. Gold-plated brass and zinc-alloy pieces are the weakest fit and the likeliest to cause "not as described" disputes. Copy must stay exact about materials ([[Voice and Rules]]) #founder
