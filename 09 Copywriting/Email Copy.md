# Email Copy

## Basics
- **From:** Lumi Paris <hello@thelumiparis.com>. Never a person's name.
- **Reply-to:** hello@thelumiparis.com (AI support: see [[Support Playbook]]).
- **Subject:** under 45 characters, sentence case, no emoji, no caps shouting, no "!". Say what is inside.
- **Preheader:** one plain sentence, under 90 characters, adds to the subject.
- **Length:** under 120 words for marketing mail. One idea, one button.
- **Button:** one, Warm Ink on Lumi Cream. Labels: "Shop the edit", "See the piece", "View your order".
- **Footer, every marketing email:** Lumi Paris · `[BUSINESS ADDRESS]` · Unsubscribe link · "You're receiving this because you joined our list." Never send without a real address.
- **Consent:** marketing mail only to people who joined the list or opted in at checkout.
- **Tone:** like the site. Plain, warm, no hustle. Sign off "Lumi Paris", no invented person.

## Flows

### 1. Early access welcome (list signup, pre-launch)
Subject: You're on the list
Preheader: We'll write when new pieces are ready.
Body: Thank you for joining. We'll write when the first pieces are ready, and only then. Lumi comes from lumière: light. Paris is how we look at jewelry, not where we're based.
Button: None, or "Follow along" to Instagram.

### 2. Launch email (store opens)
Subject: Lumi Paris is open
Preheader: Luminous everyday jewelry, meant to be lived in.
Body: The first pieces are here. Calm, quiet-glow jewelry for every day. Complimentary shipping to the US and Canada.
Button: Shop the edit

### 3. Welcome (after first purchase or signup at checkout)
Subject: Welcome to Lumi Paris
Body: Thanks for being here. Here's how we look after your jewelry: keep pearls dry, wipe plated pieces with a soft cloth, store pieces apart. Questions? Reply to this email.

### 4. Abandoned cart (one email, sent after about 4 hours, a second after 2 days at most)
Subject: Your bag
Preheader: Still here when you are.
Body: You left something in your bag. [Product name] is still there. No rush.
Button: View your bag
Rules: no discount, no "selling fast", no "only X left". If stock is truly one, you may state the fact once on the product page, never in a nudge.

### 5. Order confirmation
Subject: Your order is confirmed
Body: Thank you. We'll send tracking when your order ships. Delivery is usually 5 to 8 business days in the US and 7 to 10 in Canada. First orders can take 3 to 5 business days longer. Complimentary shipping.
Details: items, price, address.

### 6. Shipping confirmation
Subject: Your order is on its way
Body: Your order has shipped. Track it here. Delivery is usually 5 to 8 business days in the US and 7 to 10 in Canada.

### 7. Delivered and care (about 2 days after delivery)
Subject: How to care for your jewelry
Body: Your pieces should have arrived. Keep pearls dry. Wipe plated pieces with a soft cloth. If anything arrived damaged, defective or incorrect, reply here and we'll replace it. Changed your mind? You have 7 days from delivery for store credit.

### 8. Review request (about 10 days after delivery)
Subject: How are you wearing it?
Body: If you have a minute, tell us what you think of [product name]. Honest words help others choose. Reviews are shown only from real buyers.
Button: Write a review
Rules: never offer a reward for a good review. Never edit a review.

### 9. Win-back / new pieces (monthly at most)
Subject: New pieces, quietly
Body: Two new pieces this week, chosen for how they catch the light.
Button: Shop the edit
Rules: no "back in stock" or scarcity unless true and stated plainly.

### 10. Gifting season (Nov to Dec)
Subject: Give the light
Preheader: Gift-ready pieces, complimentary shipping.
Body: Pieces made to be worn every day make easy gifts. Complimentary shipping to the US and Canada. Order by [real date, from the Branvas shipping window] to arrive before Christmas.
Rules: the date must be calculated from the true delivery window and confirmed with the founder. No countdown timers.

### 11. Referral (when the mechanic and credit are decided)
Subject: Share the light
Body: Know someone who'd like Lumi Paris? Share your link. When they order, you each receive [credit amount]. Fill in only once the founder decides the amount.

## Never in email
Fake urgency, countdowns, "selling fast", "last chance", "limited", "only X left"; discount-style subject lines; all caps; emoji in subject; naming Branvas; promising cash refunds; claiming to be human; any claim not in [[Copywriting Hub]] check list.
