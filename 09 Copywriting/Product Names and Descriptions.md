# Product Names and Descriptions

## Naming system
- **Format:** [Feature] [Form], plain English. Examples: Huggie Hoops, Pearl Drop Earrings, Curb Chain Necklace, Single Stone Ring, Tennis Bracelet.
- 2 to 4 words. Title case. No "TEST".
- Name the form first, the distinguishing feature second. Stone or material word only if Branvas confirms it (pearl, zirconia, stainless steel).
- Collections and variants: add colour or metal as a variant, not in the name. "Gold-plated" and "silver" are variants.
- No French words, no invented French-style names, no Paris references, no people's names.
- No hype words in names: no "Luxe", "Signature", "Ultimate", "Perfect".
- Same form with different features: keep names parallel (Huggie Hoops, Pearl Huggie Hoops).
- A name must be unique in the catalog and short enough to fit one line on a phone.
- Keep a name list in [[Products Index]]. Check it before naming.

## Description formula (about 40 to 70 words)
1. **What it is** (1 sentence): metal, plating, stone, form. Facts only.
2. **How it wears** (1 sentence): size on the body, daily wear, layering.
3. **Light** (optional, 1 short sentence): how it catches the light. Keep to one.
4. **Care line** (1 sentence): from Branvas care data.
5. **Facts block** (metafields): `custom.material`, `custom.details`, `custom.size_guide`, filled from Branvas data only.

## Examples (illustrative: verify every fact first)
- *Gold-plated huggie hoops with a single cubic zirconia. Small enough to sleep in, bright enough to notice. Keep dry and wipe with a soft cloth.*
- *A fine curb chain in stainless steel. Layers easily with a pendant, wears alone just as well. Water-safe.* (only if Branvas marks the style water-safe)
- *Freshwater pearls on a gold-plated chain. Light on the neck, soft in the glow. Keep pearls dry.* (only if Branvas states freshwater and the plating)

## Meta and SEO
- **Title tag:** [Name] | Lumi Paris (under 60 characters).
- **Meta description (under 155):** the first sentence plus "Complimentary shipping to the US and Canada."
- **Image alt text:** describe the piece only. "Gold-plated huggie hoop earrings on cream background". Never "customer", never "real".

## Product page fixed lines
- Delivery: "Delivery 5 to 8 business days in the US, 7 to 10 in Canada. First orders can take 3 to 5 business days longer."
- Returns: "7-day returns for store credit. Damaged, defective or incorrect items are replaced."
- Shipping: "Complimentary shipping."
- Button: "Add to bag".

## Gift copy
"Gift-ready box" only if the Branvas box is the packaging we ship. Gifting lines: "Give the light." Never "perfect gift".

## Never in product copy
Everything in [[Copywriting Hub]] check list, plus: sizes or materials not on the Branvas page, "bestseller", "trending", "viral", "as seen", "customer favorite", scarcity.
