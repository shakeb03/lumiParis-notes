# About Lumi Paris

Lumi comes from lumière: light. Paris is how we look at jewelry, not where we're based.

## Light first
We start with how a piece catches the light. A small hoop by a window. A fine chain against your collarbone. A single stone that glints when you turn your head, and goes quiet when you don't. If it has to be loud to be noticed, it isn't for us.

## Meant for ordinary days
Most jewelry waits for an occasion. We think the best pieces are the ones you stop thinking about: on for the commute, the coffee, the long day, the late dinner. Layer them. Stack them. Wear them until they feel like yours.

## For you, and for the people you love
Some pieces you'll buy because you wanted them. Some you'll choose for someone else, and hope they feel seen. We keep both in mind, so every piece is described plainly and every gift is ready to give.

## Plain words, kept promises
We tell you what each piece is made of, how it wears and how to look after it. We don't dress it up.

Shipping to the US and Canada is complimentary. If something arrives damaged, defective or incorrect, we replace it free. If you change your mind, you have 7 days from delivery for store credit.

## Say hello
Questions, or a piece you'd like help choosing? Write to hello@thelumiparis.com.

Wear the light.
