# About Lumi Paris

Hello,

Lumi comes from lumière: light. Paris is how we look at jewelry, not where we're based.

Most of us have one: a necklace kept in its box for an occasion that never quite arrives. It's looked after. It's admired now and then. It's almost never worn.

Lumi Paris began with a simple thought. What if jewelry wasn't saved for anything? What if it simply went along with you?

So we look for pieces that belong in an ordinary day. The light we mean isn't a spotlight. It's window light on a Tuesday morning, soft and warm, falling on a table, a wrist, the side of your neck. A small hoop that catches it when you tuck your hair back. A fine chain that catches it when you laugh. A stone you forget about until someone says, "I like that," and you look down and smile.

Those small moments are what we're after. Not the big entrance. The ones in between.

If you're here for yourself, we hope you find something that starts to feel like part of you. If you're choosing for someone you love, we hope it's the kind of gift they put on the next morning, and keep wearing.

We're new, and we'd rather tell you so than pretend otherwise. We're still learning what you reach for. If a piece becomes part of your days, or if we've missed something you were hoping to find, write to us at hello@thelumiparis.com.

Wear the light.

Lumi Paris
