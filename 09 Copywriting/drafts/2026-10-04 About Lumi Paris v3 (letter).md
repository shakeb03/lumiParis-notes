# About Lumi Paris

Hello,

Lumi comes from lumière: light. Paris is how we look at jewelry, not where we're based.

Thank you for being here. Maybe you're shopping for yourself. Maybe it's a birthday, a thank-you, a "just because." Either way, we hope you find something you'll reach for without thinking.

That's the whole idea. We look for pieces that catch the light, then go quiet. A small hoop. A fine chain. A single stone that glints when you turn your head. Nothing loud, and nothing that waits in a drawer for a special night.

We also think you should be able to trust a jewelry shop on the small things. So here is what you'll find with us.

**Plain descriptions.** Every piece tells you what it's made of, how it wears and how to look after it. If we don't know something, we won't say it.

**One honest price.** No crossed-out numbers. No countdowns. No "only 2 left" to hurry you. If a piece is right for you, it can wait while you decide.

**Real reviews only.** Every review on this site comes from someone who bought the piece. We don't write them, and we don't invent them.

**A box worth opening.** Each order arrives in a ribboned jewelry box, wrapped in tissue, with a polishing cloth. If it's a gift, it's ready to give.

We hope the first piece you choose becomes the one people recognize you by. Wear it on the ordinary days. Those are the ones that add up.

Wear the light.

Lumi Paris
