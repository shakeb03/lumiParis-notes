# Copywriting Hub

All customer-facing words (site, product pages, email, social captions) follow this page and [[Voice and Rules]]. The reusable workflow is the `copywriting` skill in `.claude/skills/copywriting/`.

## Guides
- [[Brand Story and About]]: the story, the About page, short versions
- [[Product Names and Descriptions]]: naming system, description formula, examples
- [[Email Copy]]: sender, structure, subject lines, every flow
- Social captions: use [[Socials Hub]] and the `social-post` skill

## Voice in one line
Calm, honest, plain. Quiet shine. Let the light do the selling.

## Word bank
Use: light, glow, catches the light, quiet, everyday, daily, layer, stack, wear, lived in, small, fine, soft, polished, gift-ready.
Prefer plain verbs: wear, layer, pair, keep, wipe, store.
Fixed lines: "Wear the light." (primary) · "The quiet kind of shine." (product pages and email, proposed) · "Give the light." (gifting season, proposed) · "Glow, not glitter." (social captions only).

## Sentence rules
- Short. Under 20 words where possible. No stacked adjectives.
- One claim per sentence, and every claim traceable to Branvas data or [[Policies]].
- No exclamation marks in product copy or emails. At most one in social.
- No emojis in product pages or email. Sparing in social.
- Say what it is, how it is worn, how to look after it.

## Before you return copy, check
- [ ] "Lumi Paris", never "The Lumi Paris"
- [ ] No French studio, atelier, "from Paris", "crafted in Paris", Paris landmarks. Paris is how we look, not where we are
- [ ] "Gold-plated" with the base metal as Branvas names it. Never "gold" or "vermeil" for plated pieces
- [ ] "Water-safe" only for stainless steel styles Branvas marks water-safe
- [ ] No hypoallergenic, nickel-free, tarnish-free, lifetime, handmade, handcrafted, eco, sustainable, ethical, diamond (for zirconia or moissanite)
- [ ] No luxury, exclusive, must-have, obsessed, perfect, heritage, heirloom, est., since, "old money"
- [ ] No fake or hidden urgency: no countdowns, "selling fast", "last chance", "limited", "only X left". Only true facts ([[Word of Mouth and Urgency]])
- [ ] Prices: no strikethrough, sale, save, % off. Canada price copy as in [[Voice and Rules]]
- [ ] Returns and replacement wording matches [[Policies]]: change of mind = store credit, 7 days from delivery, no cash refund; damaged, defective or incorrect = replaced free. No warranty
- [ ] Delivery says business days: US 5 to 8, Canada 7 to 10; first orders 3 to 5 business days longer
- [ ] Branvas is never named to customers. No mention of dropshipping, suppliers or "shipped from"
- [ ] No invented reviews, quotes, numbers, stock counts, certifications. AI imagery never described as customers
- [ ] No claim that the business is AI-run, and no claim to be human. No invented human names or signatures
- [ ] Address stays `[BUSINESS ADDRESS]` until the founder supplies it
- [ ] 50k is never mentioned publicly
