# Roadmap

## Phase 1: Launch (to Fri 9 Oct 2026)
Store live, products listed, socials seeded, policies live. See [[Launch Checklist]].

## Phase 2: First sales (10 Oct to 31 Oct)
- Daily posting on Instagram, TikTok, Pinterest
- Organic first (see [[Budget]]). Ad test starts once we reach 20 orders
- First reviews (real buyers only)
- Fix store friction from first orders

## Phase 3: Scale (Nov)
- Double down on winning content and products
- Black Friday / Cyber Monday offer
- Email and abandoned cart flows

## Phase 4: Christmas push (Dec)
- Gifting collections ("Give the light."), shipping cutoff messaging
- Christmas order-by dates set from Branvas's published windows plus a safety pad, then adjusted from real delivered orders ([[Branvas]]). US 5 to 8 business days, Canada 7 to 10, first orders +3 to 5. No samples ordered
- Goal: 50k orders by Christmas 2026

## Triggers
- 20 orders: start testing ads
- 1,000 orders: run the trademark search

Track progress in [[Metrics]].
