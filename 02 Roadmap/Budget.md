## Implications
- No ad budget in this list. Organic is the primary plan (Instagram, TikTok, Pinterest). First ad test once we reach 20 orders; ad budget `[DECIDE then]`
- No sample orders. Branvas charges unit cost + $8.99 only after the customer has paid us, so the order money pays Branvas
- Shopify's first payout can arrive days after the sale while Branvas bills at order time. Keep a small float for the first orders #founder
- Change-of-mind returns cost us the piece and shipping (about unit cost + $8.99, because returned pieces can't be resold as new). At a 10% return rate and a $50 unit cost that is about $6 per order. Track the return rate in [[Metrics]]
- Free shipping means the $8.99 is a cost on every order; it must be inside the price ([[Products Index]])
