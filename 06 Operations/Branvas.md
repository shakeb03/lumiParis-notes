# Branvas (supplier)

Private-label jewelry dropshipping. Shopify order → Branvas picks, brands, packs and ships → tracking syncs to Shopify. Branvas is invisible to customers.

- **Plan:** Growth (publish up to 75 styles). Not purchased yet. Founder buys when building starts #founder
- **Account/login:** do NOT store passwords here
- **Contact:** `[TBD]` (support email on branvas.com/contact)
- **Minimum order:** none. We are charged unit cost + flat $8.99 only after the customer has paid us
- **Shipping/packaging fee:** flat $8.99 per order, duties prepaid (DDP), tracked. We absorb it: customer shipping is complimentary
- **Carrier:** USPS for US orders

## Delivery (counted from when the order is paid in Branvas, includes branding and packing)
| Region | Business days |
|---|---|

| Canada | 7 to 10 |

Branvas's own pages quote different US medians (7 to 10 days on one), so pad any Christmas order-by date beyond the published windows, and adjust from real delivered orders (no samples will be ordered).

## Packaging
Our logo on the packaging box, jewelry box and receipt. Ribboned jewelry box, tissue, polishing cloth. Custom thank-you card is VIP plan only. Nothing says Branvas.

## Returns and defects (Branvas side)
- Covered: damaged, defective, incorrect items. Not covered: change of mind, wrong size or style ordered, "doesn't match expectations"
- Claim window: 10 days from delivery (current Branvas page; an older page said 30). **We use 10 days.** Our bots forward claims the same day
- Process: the customer sends photos to us; we forward them to Branvas with the order number and description. Branvas responds within 3 business days, and an approved resolution ships or processes within 1 to 3 business days. The replacement ships directly to the customer
- Return address never shows Branvas
- Remedy options from Branvas: a replacement at no cost, or a refund as Branvas credit. **Our customer-facing remedy is a replacement** ([[Policies]])
- Returns: Branvas issues location-based return addresses; its details never appear on the label and items are never returned to our address. Who pays return shipping is not stated `[CONFIRM WITH BRANVAS]`
- No warranty published, so we offer none
- Our own policy adds change-of-mind returns for store credit within 7 days of delivery. Branvas does not reimburse these, and returned pieces cannot be resold as new, so each one costs us the piece and shipping ([[Policies]])

## Materials (as Branvas states)
Base metals: gold-plated eco-brass, S925 sterling silver, gold-plated stainless steel, eco-zinc alloy, sterling silver posts. Plating: 14K and 18K; **thickness needs the supplier spec sheet**. Stones: cubic zirconia, freshwater and baroque pearl, moissanite (lab-grown, set in S925), natural gemstones.
- Water-safe: stainless steel styles only (103 styles). Eco-brass and sterling silver are not water-safe; pearl, turquoise and shell stay dry whatever the metal
- Catalog is shared by all Branvas merchants. Our difference is curation, imagery and voice
